# W-9 Form Preparation Sheet — K1 Management LLC

Data sheet to use when filling out the official IRS Form W-9.

**IMPORTANT:** The IRS does not allow third parties to generate or sign your W-9 for you. The official, current Form W-9 must be downloaded from https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-9 and signed by an authorized officer of the LLC. This document provides the exact data to type into each field of the form.

---

## W-9 Field-by-Field Data — K1 Management LLC

**Line 1: Name (as shown on tax return)**
K1 Management LLC

**Line 2: Business name / disregarded entity name**
[Leave blank — or enter K1MLG if you operate under that DBA]

**Line 3a: Federal tax classification**
Check the box that matches your IRS election:
- Single-Member LLC owned by an individual → check "Individual/sole proprietor or single-member LLC"
- Multi-Member LLC taxed as Partnership → check "Limited liability company" and enter "P"
- LLC taxed as S-Corp → check "Limited liability company" and enter "S"
- LLC taxed as C-Corp → check "Limited liability company" and enter "C"

[CONFIRM WITH CPA — most likely "P" for partnership if multi-member, or single-member if owner-only]

**Line 3b: Foreign partner indicator**
Leave unchecked.

**Line 4: Exemptions**
Leave blank (not exempt).

**Line 5: Address**
[K1 STREET ADDRESS]

**Line 6: City, state, ZIP**
[CITY], [STATE] [ZIP]

**Line 7: Account number(s)**
Leave blank (or fill in if requested by the requester).

**Requester's name and address (optional):**
Fill in only if sending to a specific grantor that asked for it.

**Part I: Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)**
Enter your EIN: **[K1 EIN]**

(Use the EIN box, not SSN, since this is for the LLC.)

**Part II: Certification — Signature**
Signed by: [OPERATIONS LEAD NAME], [TITLE — Owner & Managing Member]

**Part II: Certification — Date**
Date of signature (today's date when you sign).

## Quick Steps to Complete

1. Download Form W-9 from irs.gov (use the most recent revision; the form is currently 1 page)
2. Open in Adobe Acrobat or any PDF editor (the IRS PDF is fillable)
3. Type each field exactly as shown in the data table above
4. Sign Part II — wet signature on a printed copy or Adobe digital signature
5. Save as `K1_Management_W9_[YYYY-MM-DD].pdf` (or `Garden_State_Motions_W9_[YYYY-MM-DD].pdf`) and upload to GrantIQ

## Notes

- If you've never confirmed how the LLC is taxed, ask your CPA — that determines Line 3.
- Most grantors will accept a W-9 dated within the last 12 months. Re-sign annually as a habit.
- Never email an unencrypted W-9. Use a secure portal, encrypted PDF, or the grantor's upload system.
