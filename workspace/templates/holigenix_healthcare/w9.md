Data sheet to use when filling out the official IRS Form W-9

IMPORTANT: The IRS does not allow third parties to generate or sign your W-9 for you. The official, current Form W-9 must be downloaded from the IRS at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-9 and signed by an authorized officer of the LLC. This document provides the exact data you will type into each field of the form so it takes you 5 minutes.

Quick Steps to Complete

Download Form W-9 from irs.gov (use the most recent revision; the form is currently 1 page).

Open in Adobe Acrobat or any PDF editor (the IRS PDF is fillable).

Type each field exactly as shown in the data table above.

Sign Part II — either with a wet signature on a printed copy or with an Adobe digital signature.

Save the file as "Holigenix_Healthcare_W9_[YYYY-MM-DD].pdf" and upload to your grant readiness portal.

Notes

If you've never confirmed how the LLC is taxed, ask your CPA — that determines Line 3.

If your address on file with the IRS differs from the suite address above, use whatever address matches your IRS records.

Most grantors will accept a W-9 dated within the last 12 months. Re-sign annually as a habit.

Never email an unencrypted W-9. Use a secure portal, encrypted PDF, or the grantor's upload system.
