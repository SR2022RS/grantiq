Governance Summary

Holigenix Healthcare LLC is governed by its Operating Agreement and managed by its Members. Decisions related to clinical operations are led by the Clinical Director. Decisions related to business operations, finance, and strategy are led by the Operations Lead. Major decisions — including significant expenditures, expansion, leadership changes, and grant participation — are made jointly by the Members.

The agency maintains current general liability and professional liability insurance, and conducts business in accordance with all applicable Georgia Department of Community Health regulations and federal healthcare laws.
