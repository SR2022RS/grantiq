How GrantIQ works

Daily intelligence. Ready-to-submit drafts.
You decide what to pursue.

Step One

Daily AI Scout

Every morning, GrantIQ scans federal (HRSA, CMS, ONC, SBIR/STTR, SAMHSA), Georgia DCH, and foundation sources for new and updated opportunities. Each result is matched against your agency profile and scored 0–100%.

Step Two

Match Scoring + Digest

Opportunities above your match threshold land in your daily email digest. You see the source, award range, deadline, eligibility requirements, and your match score — all in one email. No login required.

Step Three

AI-Drafted Narrative

When you identify an opportunity worth pursuing, GrantIQ generates a full draft application narrative based on your agency's credentials, services, certifications, and geography. You review, edit, and make it yours.

Step Four

You Submit

The final submission is always yours. GrantIQ equips you with the intelligence and the draft — you own the relationship with the funder and the decision to apply.
